Our mission

We stand up for climate justice, say no to single-use plastic and give old things a second life through upcycling and recycling.

Climate justice

We all share one planet, but climate change hits some people and places harder than others. We speak up so everyone gets a fair and healthy future.

Less single-use

Bags, straws and wrappers get used for a few minutes and then stick around for years. We choose things we can use again and again.

River's petition

Drone Show for Aberdeen

Let's keep the wonder of the Fourth of July and lose the harm. I'm asking my town to switch from fireworks to a drone show.

Sign the petition on Change.org

Why I started this petition

I'm River, and I care a lot about wildlife. For many years I loved the fireworks on the Fourth of July. Then this year I learned how much they cost the animals, the air and the people around us.

Pets run away from home. Birds panic and leave their nests. The loud bangs are hard on kids who are sensitive to sound and on veterans with PTSD. I couldn't stop thinking about it.

So I asked my town to try a drone show. Hundreds of little lights can draw hearts, animals and stories in the sky with no bangs and no smoke. It's just as magical, and it's safe for everyone and everything.

This is the biggest problem I've tried to solve so far, and I can't do it alone. Will you help me?

The facts I found

42%

Dirtier air

At more than 300 U.S. air monitors, fine-particle pollution in the 24 hours after 8 p.m. on July 4 averaged 42% higher than on nearby days. Next to one fireworks display it jumped 370%. Those tiny particles can get deep into our lungs.

30-60%

More lost pets

Animal control officials see 30% to 60% more lost pets between July 4 and July 6. July 5 is one of the busiest days of the year for animal shelters.

1,000x

Panicked birds

In a radar study in the Netherlands, about 1,000 times as many birds were flying near fireworks on New Year's Eve as on other nights. Larger birds stayed in the air for hours.

Hard on people too

Sudden bangs can be painful and scary for children and adults who are sensitive to sound and for veterans with PTSD. Smoke makes it harder for people with asthma to breathe.

Fire and litter

Sparks can start roof and brush fires. Fireworks can leave smoke, paper, plastic and metal residue behind, and some of it ends up in soil and water.

Why a drone show is better

Quiet

No booms. Pets, wildlife, babies and sound-sensitive people can all enjoy the night sky.

Safer

No explosives in the sky, so much less risk of fires and injuries. Drone shows are flown with careful planning and safety limits.

Cleaner

No smoke clouds and no paper or plastic falling into yards and water.

Creative

Hundreds of lights can make hearts, animals, the town logo or a story that changes every year.

For everyone

Families can stay together, and pets can stay safe and calm at home.

It's OK to love fireworks. I did too, for a long time. We didn't have this information before, and now that we do, we can keep the wonder and lose the harm. As Jane Goodall says, “What you do makes a difference.”

How you can help

1

Sign the petition

It takes about a minute on Change.org.

Sign now

2

Share it

Send the link to friends, neighbors and family in Aberdeen. Every share helps.

3

Speak up

Tell the Aberdeen Township Council you would love to see a drone show. Kind, polite notes and showing up to a meeting both help.

Until then, keep pets safe on July 4

  • Keep pets inside in a quiet room with music or the TV on.
  • Make sure they wear an ID tag or have a microchip.
  • Leave them at home when you go to a show.
